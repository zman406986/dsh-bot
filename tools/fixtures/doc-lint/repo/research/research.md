# Fixture research notes

Where cited research findings live. Findings go under `docs/research/` — one file per question.

Naming: short, descriptive kebab-case concept name, matching the `.out-of-scope/` convention: `dsh-interrupt-continue.md`, `dsh-todo-store.md`, `sidecar-vs-embed.md`. Recognizable from the filename alone, without opening it.

ADRs cite them: `docs/adr/0001-choose-go-for-core.md` should be able to point at `docs/research/<slug>.md` as its evidence base.
