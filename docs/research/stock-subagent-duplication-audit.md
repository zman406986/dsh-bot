## 1. The decision, stated plainly

Of the eight dispatcher tools that shipped before the swap (`src/core/policy/prompts.ts:101-111` still names the plugin's own three — `deliver_result`, `message_agent`, `spawn_dispatcher` — and the stock four cover the rest), **five go**: `spawn_worker` → stock `subagent`, `check_workers` → `list_agents`, `steer_worker` → `send_message`, `stop_worker` → `interrupt_agent`, `await_agent` → nothing (the pushed completion notice replaces it). **Three stay**: `deliver_result` (user delivery), `message_agent` (the dispatcher peer lane), `spawn_dispatcher` (the top-level peer summon of ADR-0032). The worker-local `reply_to_dispatcher` also goes, into two stock halves: an interim note is the stock `send_message` child→parent relay, and a worker's final report is its stock **closing answer**, carried by the settle notice. The plugin settle wake raised from `subagent/end` goes, and the stock `subagent-settled` notice becomes the one parent-visible result for a worker.

What stock cannot carry stays ours: worker persona/barrier composition, durable worker-session bookkeeping, the todo ledger, the peer lane, user delivery, and the summon. One behavior is **added** rather than swapped, and it is adapter-added, not a stock duplicate: a team leader pushes its answer to its summoner through the existing peer lane when it goes idle with no live workers and no queued wake. It pushes on a text change, so an interim progress idle and the later final idle can both reach the summoner; a manual `message_agent` report is deduped, a third-party peer wake does not re-arm the push, and every non-completed turn gets one stop-reason line even while a todo is still open. Stock has nothing to duplicate here (a top-level session never settles), and no host package was modified to add it.

## 3. Deliberate losses, recorded honestly — and the one that stopped being one

- **The bounded wait.** `await_agent` was the only in-turn wait primitive (0–120 s, default 30 s, one bounded wait per turn). With no stock wait tool, a dispatcher that needs a child's result before deciding must end its turn and get the notice as the next turn.
- **Status detail.** `list_agents` answers "running / inactive". Elapsed time, tool count, recent activity, transcript ref, todo id, stage and liveness verdict were `check_workers` fields and have no stock projection (`dsh-tool-subagent-control/lib/index.js:54-108` discards the service's own `activity`/`hasChildren` rows, `dsh-subagent/lib/types/list-children.js:146-151`).

**No longer a loss (revised 2026-11-16): the dispatch card.** It was recorded here as a deliberate loss on the assumption that stock `subagent` rows would fall through to the default/degraded tool row. The client change landed with the swap: the card is a PROJECTION over durable tool-call records, and `projectDispatchCard` now accepts the stock record name and maps the stock argument/result vocabulary onto the SAME card — `description` → title, `prompt` → task, optional `model`, and `{kind:"activation",subagentId}` or the bare `{subagentId}` for the outcome (`src/client/dispatch-card.ts:27,93-147`). Historical `spawn_worker` rows keep their frozen mapping, so nothing already in a transcript moves. One card, two accepted record names (`test/dispatch-card-stock.test.ts`).

## 4. What stock does not give us

- **No wait.** No stock tool blocks on a child; the only wait is host-internal.
- **No status detail.** `list_agents` is a two-state label, and it hides every non-continuable child.
- **No failed-child cause.** A local child that fails before finishing produces only "failed before it finished." — no error object, code, stack or diagnostic. Stock has no surface that would let us claim otherwise, and our copy must not imply it knows why.
- **No preset slot on the wire.** Persona/tool composition cannot be expressed through the shipped `subagent` tool config.
- **No peer lane.** Stock messaging is parent/child adjacency; a top-level dispatcher peer is unreachable.
- **No user delivery, no ledger, no per-child usage/cost.** Stock owns none of these, and none of them is replaced by the swap.

## Dispatcher visibility

**Added 2026-11-16.** Who a dispatcher may see is **ancestry plus workspace**, not the set of activated sessions. One shared predicate, `src/core/dispatcher-visibility.ts` (`canSeeDispatcherPeer`), answers both the prompt peer roster (`peerRosterFor`) and the peer lane (`message_agent` → `Multitask.sendPeer`), so the list the model is offered and the targets the tool accepts cannot drift apart.

- **Root dispatcher** (user-started; no dispatcher parent): sees its own direct children (team leaders) and other ROOT dispatchers in the same normalized cwd. It does not see grandchildren, another root's children, or another team.
- **Team leader** (summoned; a top-level session carrying our durable dispatcher mark): sees its parent, its siblings under the same parent, and its own direct children. It does not see unrelated dispatchers, another team, or its grandparent.
- **Workspace is compared on root↔root edges only.** Lineage edges inherit the spawn cwd, so parent/child/sibling checks never read it. Cross-workspace peer messaging is unintended: a missing cwd on either root fails closed (hidden), and Windows paths compare case-insensitively with separators normalized.
- **A hidden target is refused `unknown-target`**, byte-identical to a nonexistent target, so the refusal does not leak that the session exists.
- **Direct workers are not peers.** A worker is not a `message_agent` target and is not in the peer roster; it stays visible to its own parent through the ledger and stock `list_agents`.
- **Surfaces.** There is no plugin-owned UI peer list to filter: the roster snapshot and the dispatch card are worker-only, the model-facing peer list is the prompt tail, and the host-owned Agents view is out of scope.
- **`list_agents` is shadowed, not removed.** On each activated dispatcher's scope, a same-name, same-output wrapper (`src/adapter/list-agents-fence.ts`) shadows the stock verb: `descendants` is clamped to the caller's direct children (`parentId === sessionId && depth === 1`) and `children` is unchanged. It registers only when stock `list_agents` is visible in that scope and both host listing seams exist, and its disposer restores the stock definition — no host package is edited. This is **defense-in-depth, not the primary fix**: a new-style team leader is a top-level session with no catalog entry under its summoner, so stock `descendants` already ended at the caller for it; the fence closes the legacy parented-dispatcher branch, which did sit inside its parent's catalog tree.
- **Cold resume.** Lineage comes from the durable dispatcher mark's parent, not stock `header.parentSession` (a summoned leader is top-level, so its header has none). Root-to-root visibility needs a live cwd header; a dormant root already had no durable dispatcher mark and was not wake-able through the peer lane.

**Known limits.** A durable mark with no recorded parent is treated as unknown lineage and hidden from root peers (the legacy parentless shape; that guard lands in parallel with this note). A dormant root cannot be reached root-to-root because there is no live cwd to compare. The host-owned Agents view is not ours to scope.

**What this does not change.** Stock `send_message` is still parent/child adjacency, the peer lane is still ours, and the team-leader push still rides it (§2, §4). Nothing here adds a global roster or touches a host package.

## 5. ADR-0032 reconciliation

ADR-0032 records the `delivery: "parent"` removal as cleanup ("`maxDepth = parentDepth + 2` and `delivery: "parent"` disappear from the spawn path", `docs/adr/0032-summoned-dispatcher-is-a-parallel-session.md:68`) but never names what goes with it: for a **top-level summoned dispatcher** there is no activation, so `notifySettlement` never runs and nothing tells the summoner the child ended (STOCK-AUDIT §11). The adapter's idle push through the peer lane — the added behavior in §2 — restores that parent-visible terminal report, carrying the final answer and the stop/error reason. **Revised 2026-11-16:** ADR-0032's Status line now carries a dated note recording that push and the withdrawal of the "no leader final-answer push" loss. The ADR body is still not edited for that push; the push is adapter-side rather than a stock flag, and no host package was modified. Workers keep the stock notice because a worker is still a subagent activation.

## 6. What to re-check if the host upgrades

Stock here is **0.2.2-rc.1 compiled JS only**, so every `lib/*.js` line number is build-specific; the two version-sensitive seams to re-read first are the **preset gating** (`allowedAgentPresets` → `agent_preset` on the wire) and the **`list_agents` projection** (status-only fields, non-continuable children dropped).
